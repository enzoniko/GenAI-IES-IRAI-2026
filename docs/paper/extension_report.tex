\begin{abstract}
\noindent
This report documents the complete rebuild of the physics-guided
counterfactual fault-synthesis codebase and the experimental instrument
constructed to test the extension hypothesis: that synthesis fidelity in
density-guided generation is governed by the \emph{geometric compatibility}
between a fault's cluster shape in a physics-informed embedding space and the
distributional form assumed by the guidance density estimator, invariantly
(to first order) across generative backbones. We describe
(i)~\code{mafaulda\_synthetic}, a simulated rotor-bearing benchmark whose
governing equations are shared verbatim between the data generator and the
PINN physics loss, and whose seven classes form a \emph{geometry dial} that
turns cluster non-Gaussianity $\Gng_y$ into a designed, controlled
independent variable; (ii)~the corrected synthesis pipeline, including the
specific defects of the withdrawn implementation and their fixes;
(iii)~the six pre-registered experiment blocks and their decision rules; and
(iv)~the validation results already in hand --- notably that the two
scientific gates the original real-data pipeline failed (physics-embedding
class separation and non-collapsed representations) now pass with wide
margins --- versus the results awaiting the full-scale run. Colored markers
distinguish \measured{measured values} from \pending{pending ones}
throughout.
\end{abstract}

% =====================================================================
\section{Context: what actually failed, and the resulting hypothesis}
\label{sec:context}

The withdrawn conference paper~\cite{spotorno2026synthesis} proposed
physics-guided counterfactual fault synthesis via a TS-JEPA latent space, a
deterministic envelope decoder, a CVAE jitter decoder, and an SDEdit
loop~\cite{meng2022sdedit} steered by gradients through a frozen healthy-only
PINN~\cite{spotorno2026linking}. A forensic review of the code and run logs
established the concrete failure chain on real MaFaulDa data:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \textbf{Latent collapse.} The TS-JEPA encoder had no anti-collapse
mechanism; real-data latents collapsed to per-dimension standard deviation
$\sim\!10^{-6}$, so every train-on-synthetic/test-on-real (TSTR) probe scored
exactly chance.
\item \textbf{Physics mismatch.} The PINN's rotor equations and normalization
were calibrated for a different operating point; residual-feature clustering
reached silhouette $0.006$--$0.079$, far below the $0.3$ gate.
\item \textbf{Silent physics bypass.} The reported ``physics-informed''
results were produced with \code{ORACLE\_MODE="raw"} --- the feature extractor
applied to raw signals, no PINN in the loop.
\item \textbf{Degenerate guidance.} The SDEdit implementation computed
$t_{\mathrm{start}} = \lfloor \text{n\_infer} \times \text{strength} \rfloor
= \lfloor 10 \times 0.1062\rfloor = 1$: the ``guided diffusion'' performed a
single reverse step. The penalty was an MSE to a class-mean embedding, not
the Mahalanobis density the paper described; no covariance existed in the
repository.
\end{enumerate}

The extension reframes the salvageable observation --- impulsive faults
reproduced well, multi-harmonic faults poorly --- as a hypothesis:

\paragraph{Hypothesis (H).} \emph{In density-guided synthesis steered toward
a target distribution in a physics-informed embedding space, fidelity is
primarily determined by the compatibility between the fault's true cluster
geometry and the distributional form assumed by the guidance density
estimator, rather than by backbone capacity or guidance strength; to first
order this relationship is invariant to the generative backbone.}

\paragraph{Why synthetic-first.} On real data, cluster geometry $\Gng_y$ is
observational and confounded. On a simulated system we \emph{manipulate} it:
classes are constructed whose embedding clusters are unimodal-Gaussian,
bimodal, heavy-tailed, skewed, elongated, or ring-shaped by design
(Sec.~\ref{sec:dial}), turning the primary test (C3) from a correlation
study into a near-causal experiment. Real MaFaulDa remains a config flag
(\code{dataset=mafaulda}) for the subsequent phase.

\section{The \code{mafaulda\_synthetic} benchmark}
\label{sec:benchmark}

\subsection{Healthy physics: one source of truth}
A two-mass, two-plane lumped rotor-bearing model in deviation coordinates
around static equilibrium:
\begin{align}
M_2\ddot{x}_2 + D_2\dot{x}_2 + K_1 x_2 + K_c(x_2-x_3) &= M_1\omega^2 E_1\cos(\omega t + \varphi_0),\nonumber\\
M_2\ddot{y}_2 + D_2\dot{y}_2 + K_{1y}(t)\, y_2 + K_c(y_2-y_3) &= M_1\omega^2 E_1\sin(\omega t + \varphi_0),\nonumber\\
M_3\ddot{x}_3 + D_3\dot{x}_3 + K_2 x_3 + K_c(x_3-x_2) &= 0,\nonumber\\
M_3\ddot{y}_3 + D_3\dot{y}_3 + K_2 y_3 + K_c(y_3-y_2) &= F_{\mathrm{imp}}(t).
\label{eq:ode}
\end{align}
Healthy regime: $\varphi_0=0$, isotropic constant $K_{1y}\equiv K_1$,
$F_{\mathrm{imp}}\equiv 0$. Observables are the four accelerations
$(\ddot{x}_2,\ddot{y}_2,\ddot{x}_3,\ddot{y}_3)$ --- the same four-channel
format as processed MaFaulDa. Both the simulator (batched RK4, $2\times$
oversampled, initialized at the closed-form harmonic steady state) and the
PINN residual loss import Eq.~\eqref{eq:ode} from the same module
(\code{src/physics/rotor\_model.py}), so ``the PINN can fit the data'' holds
\emph{by construction} --- the property the real-data attempt never had.
Nominal parameters place the structural modes at
\measured{162 and 202\,Hz} with damping ratio $\approx 0.04$; a detuned
``machine B'' variant supports the cross-dataset experiment E4.

\subsection{Measurement model and windowing}
$\x = \x_{\mathrm{clean}} + \boldsymbol{\varepsilon}$, where
$\x_{\mathrm{clean}}$ is the ODE acceleration and $\boldsymbol{\varepsilon}$
is broadband Gaussian noise (5\% of healthy RMS) plus a 5\,kHz hum (10\%),
both stored separately (exact ground truth for disentanglement experiments).
Sampling: $f_s = 50$\,kHz, $T=4167$ samples ($=$ one revolution at the
slowest speed), speeds $\{12, 16, 20\}$\,Hz with $\pm 0.5\%$ per-window
jitter, keyphasor-aligned windows, independent draws per window (no temporal
leakage between splits by construction). Velocity/position inputs for the
PINN are derived from acceleration by \emph{synchronous} integration
(offset removal over an integer number of revolutions using the measured
$\omega$) --- identical at training and guidance time.

\subsection{The geometry dial}
\label{sec:dial}
Seven classes; each is a physical mechanism \emph{plus a sampling law over
its fault parameters}; the sampling law shapes the class's embedding-cluster
geometry:

\begin{table}[h]\centering\small
\begin{tabular}{llll}
\toprule
\# & Class & Mechanism / sampling law & Designed geometry\\
\midrule
0 & \code{healthy} & nominal & tight unimodal\\
1 & \code{imbalance\_uni} & $\kappa\!\sim\!\mathcal{N}(6,0.3)$ on $E_1$ & unimodal (low $\Gng$)\\
2 & \code{imbalance\_bi} & $(\kappa,\varphi_0)\!\sim\!\tfrac12\mathcal{N}((4,0))+\tfrac12\mathcal{N}((9,\tfrac{\pi}{2}))$ & bimodal (high $\Gng$)\\
3 & \code{bpfo\_impulsive} & impact train, amp $\sim$ LogNormal & heavy-tailed\\
4 & \code{misalign\_cont} & $K_{1y}$ anisotropy $+2X$, $s\!\sim\!U(0.05,0.5)$ & elongated continuum\\
5 & \code{looseness\_skew} & $K_{1y}$ dropouts, duty $\sim$ Beta$(2,8)$ & skewed\\
6 & \code{combo\_ring} & $\kappa=6$, $\varphi_0\!\sim\!U(0,2\pi)$ & ring manifold\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{Classes 1 and 2 share the mechanism and differ \emph{only} in the
sampling law --- the matched pair at the heart of H.}
\end{table}

\section{The corrected pipeline}
\label{sec:pipeline}

\textbf{Representation.} TS-JEPA~v2: masked latent prediction plus explicit
anti-collapse --- a VICReg-style variance/covariance penalty on the pooled
$\z\in\mathbb{R}^{128}$ (always on) and an optional SIGReg/Epps--Pulley
term~\cite{balestriero2025lejepa} for the A2 arm. Decoder~1 reconstructs the
\emph{clean} envelope (ground truth available); Decoder~2 is a conditional
VAE~\cite{sohn2015cvae} on the jitter residual with an optional
$\beta$-TCVAE total-correlation penalty~\cite{chen2018isolating} (E1 arm).

\textbf{Physics-informed embedding.} The frozen healthy-only PINN maps a
window to 12 channels --- 4 corrective forces, 4 physics residuals of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:ode} evaluated on the measured kinematics, 4 data residuals ---
which a fully differentiable feature extractor (9 time statistics, 256 FFT
magnitudes, Haar wavelet statistics per channel; $F=3360$) followed by an
\emph{unwhitened} PCA projection maps to $\bv\in\mathbb{R}^{32}$. (Whitening
was measured to destroy class separation, silhouette $0.42\!\to\!0.17$;
density estimators handle scale internally.) A diagnostic
\code{oracle.mode=raw} bypass still exists, but every artifact it touches is
tagged \code{bypass=true} in the run manifest and the report aggregator
refuses to mix such rows into headline tables --- the guardrail that makes
failure~(3) of Sec.~\ref{sec:context} structurally unrepeatable.

\textbf{Guidance densities (the estimator family of H).} A common interface
(\code{fit}, differentiable \code{log\_prob}/\code{penalty},
\code{held\_out\_ll}) with three members: single Gaussian with Ledoit--Wolf
shrinkage~\cite{ledoit2004well} (the Mahalanobis penalty the withdrawn paper
\emph{claimed}), a full-covariance GMM with BIC-selected $k\le 4$, and a
RealNVP normalizing flow~\cite{papamakarios2021normalizing} that refuses to
fit below 20 samples and records a Gaussian fallback (a B3 finding, not a
silent failure).

\textbf{Corrected guided SDEdit (ours backbone).}
$t_0 = \lfloor t_{0,\mathrm{frac}} \cdot 1000\rfloor$ on the \emph{training}
schedule (default 500); DDIM~\cite{song2021ddim} sub-sampled schedule of
$n_{\mathrm{infer}}$ steps; every $N$ steps a guidance event computes
$\mathcal{L} = -\log q_y\!\big(\Feat(\PINN(\mathcal{D}_1(\z_t)))\big)$ and
applies the norm-clipped gradient after the DDIM step; per-step penalty and
gradient norms are logged (StepTrace, feeds D3). The diffusion operates on
\emph{standardized} latents (scale factor stored with the checkpoint) ---
without this the reverse process measurably drifted off-manifold.

\textbf{External backbones.} A signal-space conditional VAE (independent
mechanism; consumes guidance by density-weighted resampling) and a
FaultDiffusion-style~\cite{xu2025faultdiffusion} few-shot model (frozen
healthy-only diffusion backbone plus per-class low-rank difference adapters;
reimplemented --- no public code exists --- with the shared-latent-space
confound documented).

\begin{tabular}{p{0.31\textwidth}p{0.33\textwidth}p{0.27\textwidth}}
\toprule
Withdrawn-implementation defect & Fix & Evidence\\
\midrule
$t_{\mathrm{start}}=1$ (guidance effectively off) & $t_0$ on training schedule; DDIM sub-schedule & sanity gates, Sec.~\ref{sec:validated}\\
MSE-to-mean ``guidance'' & density family $-\log q_y$, Ledoit--Wolf/GMM/Flow & unit tests order estimators correctly on designed toys\\

\section{Experimental design and pre-registered rules}
\label{sec:design}

\textbf{Operationalization.} For each (class, speed) cell,
$\Gng_y$ is computed on the real (simulated) windows' embeddings in the
cluster's own top-8 principal subspace, with three statistics reported:
Henze--Zirkler~\cite{henze1990class} (primary), Mardia
skewness/kurtosis~\cite{mardia1970measures}, and the Epps--Pulley energy
statistic (the SIGReg objective). Fidelity per cell: per-class
MMD~\cite{gretton2012kernel} between generated and real test windows
(primary) and per-class TSTR recall~\cite{esteban2017real}, with TSTR
computed on a fixed backbone-agnostic feature map of raw windows, labels
taken from the generation condition, probes $\in$ \{logistic, 1-NN,
RBF-SVM\}, and the oracle trained on real data.

\textbf{Blocks.} A: disentanglement controls ($I(\z;\boldsymbol{\varepsilon})$
via MINE~\cite{belghazi2018mine} against a plain-AE baseline; SIGReg arm;
envelope/jitter spectral coherence). B (core): estimator $\times$ backbone
$\times$ class $\times$ speed $\times$ seed factorial, plus the few-shot
sweep $n\in\{5,10,20,50,100\}$ with held-out log-likelihood reported per fit
(bias-vs-variance confound control). C: geometry characterization and
\textbf{C3}, the primary geometry$\to$fidelity correlation. D: guidance
interval, $t_0$, gradient-norm diagnostic, multi-speed. E: $\beta$-TCVAE,
latent modality/class-conditional capacity intervention, open-set synthesis
by density interpolation (with distance to the \emph{true} held-out cluster
--- measurable only on synthetic data), cross-machine zero-shot. F:
multi-seed benchmark, probe-sensitivity, causal ablation (unguided and
wrong-target-guided controls).

\textbf{Decision rules (pre-registered, encoded in
\code{src/evaluation/correlate.py}).} H is supported per condition if the
Spearman correlation between $\Gng_y$ and fidelity across (class, speed)
cells satisfies $|\rho|\ge 0.4$ with consistent sign; backbone invariance is
graded (a) comparable sign and strength across backbones, (b) same sign only,
(c) single-backbone; otherwise rejected. Capacity/strength interventions
(D1, E2) must \emph{fail} to close the high-$\Gng_y$ gap for the causal
reading to stand.

\section{Validation results already in hand}
\label{sec:validated}

All numbers below are \measured{measured} (reduced-scale configuration
\code{gate\_v2}: 100/50/80 windows per class$\times$speed, three speeds,
single seed unless noted) and reproducible from run manifests.

\subsection{Instrument soundness (unit-level, 32 automated tests)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=1pt]
\item Simulator satisfies its own healthy equations to \measured{$<10^{-6}$}
relative residual; steady-state amplitude matches the closed-form frequency
response to \measured{$<1\%$}; structural modes inside $[80,400]$\,Hz.
\item Synchronously derived kinematics keep the healthy residual below
\measured{$0.05\%$} of the forcing amplitude at all three speeds
(measured \measured{$0.03\%$}); fault classes exceed the healthy residual by
\measured{$>100\times$}.
\item Dataset generation is bit-deterministic given (config, seed); spectral
signatures verified (1X boost for imbalance, 2--3\,kHz band for BPFO, $2X$
for misalignment).
\item Guidance estimators: GMM and Flow beat the Gaussian by $>1$ nat
held-out log-likelihood on designed bimodal toys; all penalties
differentiate; $n=5$ fits do not crash; the flow's few-shot fallback engages.
\item Metric sanity: TSTR $=$ oracle when generated $=$ real; MMD $\to 0$ on
identical distributions; $\Gng$ orders Gaussian $<$ bimodal; the C3
correlation and invariance-grading machinery recover a planted effect.
\item MINE recovers the analytic MI of correlated Gaussians within
\measured{$0.25$ nats} and $\approx 0$ for independent pairs.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Gate 1: the physics-informed embedding separates classes}
The gate the real-data pipeline failed (silhouette $0.006$--$0.079$) now
passes with margin: per-speed silhouette \measured{$0.436$} (gate: $\ge
0.3$), 5-NN accuracy \measured{$0.974$} (gate: $\ge 0.9$), PINN validation
data-MSE \measured{$0.0033$} (standardized units).

\subsection{Gate 2: the geometry dial produces its designed ordering}
Henze--Zirkler $\Gng_y$ per class (mean over speeds, class-local subspace):

\begin{center}\small
\begin{tabular}{lccccccc}
\toprule
 & healthy & misalign & bpfo & loose & ring & uni & bi\\
\midrule
$\Gng_y$ (HZ) & \measured{1.28} & \measured{2.20} & \measured{3.05} &
\measured{4.26} & \measured{4.56} & \measured{8.19} & \measured{17.55}\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

All pre-registered dial checks pass: healthy most Gaussian, bimodal least,
matched pair \code{bi}$>$\code{uni} at every speed, and an
\measured{$8\times$} spread across fault classes --- ample variance in the
independent variable of H. (Note: \code{imbalance\_uni} measures higher than
naively designed because the $(\kappa,\omega)$ response manifold curves near
resonance; reducing speed jitter to $\pm0.5\%$ bounded but did not eliminate
this. The matched-pair contrast that H needs is preserved.)

\subsection{Component and mechanism checks}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=1pt]
\item TS-JEPA v2 does not collapse: validation $\sigma_z=$
\measured{$0.71$} per dimension, effective rank \measured{$18.9$}
(v1 on real data: $\sigma_z\sim 10^{-6}$).
\item Decoder~1 keeps \measured{$3.4\%$} of output energy above 4\,kHz
(envelope excludes the jitter band); Decoder~2's KL term is
\measured{$5.96 > 0$} (non-collapsed posterior).
\item Guided SDEdit, causal direction: with $t_0$-fraction $0.05$ the
unguided edit round-trips at the reconstruction floor (MSE
\measured{$1.3$} vs.\ \measured{$0.26$} direct reconstruction $+$ jitter
variance); guidance toward \code{imbalance\_uni} monotonically decreases the
penalty (\measured{$582\to119$}) and grows the 1X spectral line from
\measured{$77\mathrm{k}$} (healthy) to \measured{$271\mathrm{k}$}
(vs.\ \measured{$150\mathrm{k}$} unguided) --- the guidance signal steers the
physically correct feature, which the withdrawn implementation could not do.
\item End-to-end: all six blocks execute and write analysis-ready cells at
smoke and reduced scale; at reduced scale (60-epoch training, 50 generated
windows/class, untuned $t_0$/scale) the TSTR/oracle ratio is
\measured{$0.29$} with oracle accuracy \measured{$0.97$} --- reported here
only as an execution proof, not as evidence about H.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:repro}

Single entry point; every run writes a manifest (resolved config, git SHA,
seed, outputs); headline tables regenerate from manifests only.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=1pt]
\item Tests: \code{python -m pytest} (32 tests).
\item Smoke: \code{python run.py smoke-all} ($\sim$10 min, every stage and block).
